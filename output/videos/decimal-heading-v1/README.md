These MP4s show the first five seed-2026 development scenarios, in order, with
the selected models and decimal heading transport revision 1. They are simulator
recordings and do not represent population scores or full official evaluations.

- sa-first-five.mp4: one controlled aircraft, 38.4 seconds.
- ma-first-five.mp4: ten controlled aircraft, 44.8 seconds.

Both deployments reproduce all nine prior recorded metrics exactly for every
aircraft in these scenarios (5 SA records, 50 MA records). The scenario random
streams are unchanged during recording. Both MP4s decode completely, preserve
the source durations, and have readable in-flight and final outcome cards.
Each track's audit records model, deployment, source-reel and video hashes.

The SA preview retains its conflicts, including 78 and 41 seconds of intrusion
in scenarios 2 and 5. A waypoint arrival must not be described as a safe arrival.
The older videos in frozen-original-v1 remain the original-format evidence.
